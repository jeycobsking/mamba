Sanctuary History & Mission

Preserving East African Reptiles Since 1996

Mamba Village Nairobi is widely recognized as Kenya’s premier reptile conservation facility. Situated on the scenic expanse of Karen/Langata, the park balances eco-tourism with active conservation of threatened Nile crocodiles and endangered indigenous reptiles.

Frequently Asked Questions

Visitor Information & Guidelines

What are the peak crocodile feeding times? +
Crocodile feeding is conducted every day at 2:30 PM and 4:30 PM. We recommend arriving at least 30 minutes prior to secure prime observation spots on the upper gallery.
How do digital QR tickets work at the entry gate? +
Upon completing your M-Pesa payment, a digital ticket with a unique scannable QR code is instantly saved to your browser wallet and emailed to you. You can either show it directly on your smartphone screen or print it.
Is parking available on site? +
Yes! Secure, gated parking with dedicated security wardens is available free of charge for all ticketed guests and school tour buses.
Can we host private events, birthdays, or weddings at the park? +
Absolutely. Our manicured gardens and banquet gazebos can host up to 600 attendees. You can request a quote via the Group Booking section or contact our Service Manager directly.
